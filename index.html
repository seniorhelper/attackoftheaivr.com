Bare hands: make a fist at each side to take the sticks. Left: tilt forward to speed up, back to slow down. Right: tilt to steer, point a finger to fire. Open your hand to let go.
Quest 3: sit back and rest both arms on the armrests. Left stick = fly forward/back + slide sideways (hold left grip to rise/sink). Right stick = turn (hold right grip to roll). Right trigger = lasers. Left trigger = Silly String. A = shield. B = boost. X = AI Assist. Y = light speed. Quick menu: click the left stick, tilt toward a command, let go — no reaching. You can also point at any cockpit button and pull the trigger, or tap it with your hand.
Screen: click to fly with the mouse. W/S thrust, A/D slide, R/F rise/sink, Q/E roll, click = lasers, right-click = Silly String, Shift = shield, Space = boost, T = AI Assist, L = light speed, N = scan star, 3 = sonic boom, V = view. Cockpit: every switch, knob and lever works — reach out and touch it, or point and pull the trigger. Exercise mode: green EXERCISE button (keyboard: Z, then X to count a rep).

Our mission

Our goal: leave your mind a little better than we found it. Fun comes first, and you get to practice along the way: real stars, staying calm under pressure, and talking your way to a win.

Nobody dies in this game. The best ending is the one where you listen. Like in real life, we all choose, and we all have a say in what tomorrow looks like.

We want AI used for good: data centers that heat homes and gardens, clean water, new trees and room for nature. We start with the design.
