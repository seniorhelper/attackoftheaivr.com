# Attack of the AI

A seated VR space-combat game for Meta Quest 3, played right in the browser. No install.

**Play:** https://www.attackoftheaivr.com — open it in the Quest browser and press ENTER VR, or play on a desktop with mouse and keyboard.

*Leaving minds better than we found them.*

- Fly a cockpit where every switch, knob and lever works
- Rebel AI machines, a black hole into a neon dimension, a planet rover mission and a Boss AI you can fight or talk down
- Family friendly: machines get rebooted, nobody gets hurt
- Seated exercise mode with a Drill Android and a calorie estimate
- Built to stay well under photosensitive-seizure flash limits

All models, textures, sounds and code are original. Star data: HYG catalog (CC BY-SA 4.0). Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble. Engine: three.js (MIT).
